Discovery & User Insights

User interviews with job seekers and early-career professionals highlighted two core barriers in oral communication:

Emotional Barrier

Fear of human judgment during live mock interviews prevents consistent daily practice.

Functional Barrier

Lack of objective metrics on pacing, filler words, clarity, and structural response quality.

AI Feedback Engine Architecture

Designed a resilient multi-stage evaluation pipeline to guarantee consistent feedback without hallucination:

1 User Audio Stream → STT (Speech-to-Text Transcribe)
2 Pacing & Filler Word Extraction (Regex & WPM Counters)
3 LLM Structured Analysis (Prompt Guardrails for Clarity & STAR Format)
4 Fallback Handling for Low Audio & Ambiguous Transcripts
